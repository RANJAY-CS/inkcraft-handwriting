How to Create Your Own Handwriting Font

You can easily turn your real physical handwriting into a font file (.ttf or .otf) for free in about 5 minutes:

1 Visit a free font generator like Calligraphr.com or YourFonts.com.
2 Print or digitally draw on their quick template sheet (writing letters A–Z and numbers in your normal pen style).
3 Take a photo or scan of the sheet and upload it to the site.
4 Download your generated .ttf or .otf font file!

Once downloaded, click the "Upload Font File" button in InkCraft, select your file, and your exact handwriting will immediately render on the page!
